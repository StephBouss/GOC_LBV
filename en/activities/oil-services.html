Oil services

National technical expertise

With SMP, GOC carries out in-house the technical services its production sites need.

Our subsidiary

SMP: a 100% Gabonese subsidiary

The takeover of the Société de Maintenance Pétrolière (SMP) is a lever of GOC's industrial strategy. The Group now has a subsidiary entirely dedicated to oil technical services, which strengthens its command of drilling, maintenance and technical support activities.

  • Mechanics, instrumentation, electrical work and industrial maintenance
  • Drilling, maintenance and technical support at production sites
  • Less dependence on outside contractors
  • Stronger Gabonese skills
Discover SMP

Our areas of work

Technical trades that support production

SMP works on oil facilities, from routine maintenance to support for drilling operations.

Mechanics and industrial maintenance

Servicing and overhauling equipment at production sites to keep it available and safe.

Instrumentation and electrical work

Installing, testing and repairing the electrical networks and measuring instruments of the facilities.

Drilling and technical support

Support for drilling operations and technical assistance to teams, as close as possible to the production sites.

Operational sovereignty

What bringing these services in-house changes

By carrying out these services itself, GOC reduces its dependence on outside contractors and builds up cutting-edge local skills.

01

Cost control

Carrying out maintenance and drilling within the Group reduces the amounts paid to outside contractors.

02

Quality of work

Teams who know the Group's facilities and follow its safety requirements.

03

Responsiveness on site

Technicians available more quickly at production sites, to limit shutdowns.

04

Local content

A 100% Gabonese subsidiary that trains people and develops the country's technical skills.
