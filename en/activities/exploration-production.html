Exploring, producing and growing national reserves

Upstream oil is the foundation of the Group's growth.

Upstream

Exploration & Production

In just a few years, SNHG has become a major player in national production. Its own production, from its operated and non-operated assets, now stands at around 27,000 barrels per day. This momentum is reinforced by the performance of Assala Energy's assets and by the strategic interests managed by Gabon Oil Participations (GOP).

  • Own production of around 27,000 barrels per day
  • Nearly 90,000 barrels per day of consolidated production
  • Operator of the offshore Ayol, Konzi Sardine and Olowui permits and the onshore Akondo, Mboga II, Dinonga Irondou and Remboué permits
  • Interests in permits operated by ASSALA, BWE, Maurel & Prom, Perenco and TotalEnergies EP Gabon

Exploration

Exploration is the bedrock of our business. Our strategy relies on acquiring high-resolution seismic data and on targeted drilling campaigns, in partnership with the world's leading players.

Production

Hydrocarbon production is managed by our subsidiaries and partners under SNHG's rigorous oversight. We optimise the operation of mature fields while developing new infrastructure.

Safety & environment

Safety and compliance with HSSE standards are at the heart of every barrel produced, with the aim of minimising environmental risks.
