Why join us?

6 good reasons to build your professional future at GOC

Joining GOC

Joining Gabon Oil Company (GOC) means putting your skills at the service of a national ambition: producing, managing and adding value to Gabon's hydrocarbon resources, while contributing to the country's economic development.

It also means choosing a demanding professional environment, where know-how grows, responsibilities are shared and every role contributes to a common ambition.

Discover six reasons to build your career with us.

01

Pride in serving Gabon

Give meaning to your professional commitment by helping to add value to national resources and to build a strong, sustainable Gabonese oil industry.

02

Skills that grow

Develop your expertise through training, shared experience, mentoring and continuous learning, in close contact with the technical and operational realities of our sector.

03

People at the heart of our development

Join a company that recognises the value of skills, encourages career progression and sees its employees as key contributors to its performance.

04

Projects that matter

Take part in major industrial and energy projects whose stakes reach beyond the company and contribute to Gabon's economic development and energy sovereignty.

05

Safety as a daily requirement

Work in an environment where health, occupational safety and environmental protection guide practices, decisions and professional conduct.

06

The strength of the team

Join teams where diverse professions, mutual support and knowledge sharing make it possible to meet operational challenges and grow together.

At GOC, your expertise has its place. Your commitment contributes to a national ambition.

Let's build Gabon's energy future together.
