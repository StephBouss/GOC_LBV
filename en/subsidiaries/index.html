Subsidiaries

Subsidiaries and entities

SNHG's integrated ecosystem serving Gabon's energy sovereignty.

Our subsidiaries

Players across the value chain

In fifteen years, the Société Nationale des Hydrocarbures du Gabon (SNHG) has gradually built an integrated group covering the entire oil value chain. This growth rests on five complementary subsidiaries, which are now the main drivers of its development.

Upstream

Assala Energy

Acquired in June 2024, Assala Energy is a major strategic asset, producing around 57,000 barrels per day.

Visit the official website (new tab)

Gabon Oil Company (GOC)

SNHG's parent entity and strategic hub, ensuring the consistency and steering of all its subsidiaries.

Parent entity

Gabon Oil Participations (GOP)

Integration of Tullow Oil Gabon's interests into the national strategy to consolidate the oil portfolio.

Group information

Downstream

Gab'Oil

A network of 12 service stations and a key player in national energy security, distributing fuel throughout Gabon.

Official page (new tab)

Pizolub

Modernised Port-Gentil site producing lubricants that meet international industrial quality standards.

Visit the official website (new tab)

Oil services

SMP – Société de Maintenance Pétrolière

Subsidiary dedicated to oil technical services: mechanical engineering, instrumentation, electrical work and industrial maintenance.
