Our subsidiaries

One group. One integrated energy value chain.

In fifteen years, Gabon Oil Company (GOC) has gradually built an integrated group covering the entire oil value chain, from upstream to downstream. This growth rests on five complementary subsidiaries, which are now the main drivers of its development.

  1. Upstream

    • Gabon Oil Company
    • Assala Energy
    • Gabon Oil Participations

  2. Upstream Oilfield Services

    • SMP – Société de Maintenance Pétrolière

  3. Midstream

    • Gab'Oil
    • Gabon Oil Company

  4. Downstream

    • Gab'Oil
    • Pizolub

Subsidiaries and entities

Players across the value chain

From oil fields to the service station network, each entity in the Group holds a link in the chain.

Upstream · Midstream

Gabon Oil Company (GOC)

The Group's parent entity and strategic hub, ensuring the consistency and steering of all its subsidiaries.

Parent entity

  • Upstream

    Assala Energy

    Acquired in June 2024, Assala Energy is a major strategic asset, producing around 57,000 barrels per day.

    SMP – Société de Maintenance Pétrolière
