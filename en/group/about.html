About the Group

About us

The leading player in Gabon's oil sector, GOC works for national energy sovereignty.

A word from the Managing Director

Transforming our resources, lighting up our future

GOC is now at the heart of a decisive stage for Gabon's energy sovereignty. By regaining control of major assets and consolidating our subsidiaries, we have made the strategic choice to give the Nation back control of its most precious resources.

Our ambition is clear: to turn hydrocarbons into lasting national wealth, economic stability and opportunities for future generations.

As the operational arm of the State, we work every day to modernise our industry, strengthen our capabilities and raise GOC to the ranks of the leading oil players on the continent.

I commend the commitment of our teams and the trust placed in us by the highest authorities. Together, we are building a stronger, more responsible GOC that is resolutely focused on the future.

Marcellin SIMBA NGABIManaging Director

Missions

GOC: independent, strategic management

Managing the State's interests

Managing the State's interests in oil activities and the Nation's strategic assets, and securing revenue from oil production.

Marketing petroleum products

Marketing petroleum products and, through Gab'Oil, ensuring supply and distribution across the country.

Operating hydrocarbon fields

Operating hydrocarbon fields on behalf of the Nation, including the direct operation of strategic permits such as Dinonga and Irondou.

Driving a responsible energy transition

Deploying low-carbon technologies and diversifying the Group into the energies of the future, in support of the Nation's energy independence.

Vision

Building a strong and dynamic GOC

Building a strong and dynamic national hydrocarbons company that makes a lasting contribution to the economy and development of the Gabonese Republic.

Values

  • Respect

    Compliance with standards and due consideration for people as key drivers of productivity.

  • Integrity

    Honesty in day-to-day management and a guaranteed healthy and fair working environment.

  • Excellence

    A constant drive to deliver the best service and to keep improving.

  • Teamwork

    Complementarity and collaboration to meet the challenge of competitiveness.

  • Profitability

    Accountability for results and for the brand image, through engaging management.

  • Innovation

    Constantly adopting new technologies and methods to ensure sustainable growth.
