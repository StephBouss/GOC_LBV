Transformation

Modernising management tools and transforming the organisation

A wide-ranging internal modernisation programme to strengthen overall performance.

November 2025·1 min read

SNHG has launched a wide-ranging internal modernisation programme to strengthen its overall performance. This transformation rests first and foremost on the digitalisation of processes, which automates many tasks, shortens operational lead times and improves data reliability.

The company is also optimising its technical data flows, a key issue for obtaining real-time information on oil and gas operations.

However, this internal transformation cannot succeed without upskilling the teams. SNHG is therefore rolling out continuous training programmes.
