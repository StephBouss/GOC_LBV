Who we are

GOC: driving energy sovereignty

Gabon Oil Company (GOC) is the State's operational instrument for managing the nation's oil and gas assets. Founded in August 2011, it embodies Gabon's sovereign choice to take charge of its energy future.

  • Missions

    Managing the State's interests in oil activities, marketing petroleum products and operating hydrocarbon fields on behalf of the Nation.

  • Vision

    Building a strong and dynamic national hydrocarbons company that makes a lasting contribution to the economy and development of the Gabonese Republic.

  • Values

    Respect, integrity, excellence, teamwork, profitability and innovation.

Sustainable strategy

Building a responsible energy future

At GOC, industrial performance goes hand in hand with our commitment to people and nature.

  • HSEI

    Hygiene, Health, Safety, Environment and Integrity: the health and safety of our employees and partners, and the protection of the environment, are the fundamental pillars of our operations.

  • CSR

    Corporate Social Responsibility: GOC is committed to contributing to the well-being of Gabonese communities and to promoting sustainable development in all its areas of operation.
