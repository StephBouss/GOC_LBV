HSEI commitments

Protecting people, controlling risks

At GOC, no industrial performance can ever justify compromising on people's safety, environmental protection or the reliability of our facilities.

Our approach

HSEI at the heart of our decisions and operations

Aware of the specific demands of the oil industry, Gabon Oil Company (GOC) puts Hygiene, Health, Safety, the Environment and Integrity (HSEI) first. Rigorous policies, constant risk prevention and individual accountability all serve the same ambition:

  • protecting life;
  • preserving our environment;
  • ensuring safe, responsible and sustainable operations.

From employee safety to equipment integrity, through road safety and compliance with the 10 Golden Rules, this ambition is organised around four pillars.

Our four pillars of commitment

Four pillars for safe operations

Each pillar sets precise requirements, from risk prevention on site to the travel of our teams.

01

Hygiene, health, safety and environment (HSSE)

Preventing risks, protecting lives, preserving the environment.

Senior management, employees and partners are united around a culture of prevention, vigilance and continuous improvement. Every accident avoided is a collective victory.

  • Risk identification and assessment
  • Staff training and regulatory compliance
  • Prevention of accidents and occupational diseases, emergency preparedness
  • Emissions control, waste management and site rehabilitation
  • Zero tolerance for alcohol and drugs on site
02

Safe driving

Making every journey a commitment to safety.

Risks do not stop at the gates of our facilities. A successful journey is not measured by its speed, but by everyone arriving at their destination without an accident.

  • Valid licence and defensive driving training
  • Well-maintained vehicles, speed limits respected, seat belts compulsory
  • No substances and no phones at the wheel
  • Rest periods, driving adapted to the weather, incidents reported
03

Facility integrity and process safety

Anticipating failures, securing facilities, sustaining production.

From design to maintenance, the reliability of equipment determines people's safety, the protection of investments and the continuity of operations.

  • Preventive hazard identification and risk studies
  • Monitoring of safety-critical equipment
  • Control of operating parameters
  • Qualified personnel, inspections, audits and lessons learned
04

Our 10 Golden Rules

Ten essential rules, one absolute priority: life.

Our safety requirements translated into concrete behaviours, to be applied every day on site and during operations. Prevention depends as much on rigorous procedures as on everyone's sense of responsibility.

Discover the 10 Golden Rules

Our 10 Golden Rules

One absolute priority: life

These rules are essential guidelines for employees, service providers and subcontractors. Following them strengthens operational discipline, prevents dangerous situations and builds a shared safety culture.

  1. 01Drive safely
  2. 02Hold a valid work permit
  3. 03Wear personal protective equipment (PPE)
  4. 04Protect yourself against falls when working at height
  5. 05Caution, danger: chemicals
  6. 06Plan lifting operations and control the perimeter
  7. 07Isolate equipment before any intervention
  8. 08Follow the rules for working in confined spaces
  9. 09Use the right tools
  10. 10Never walk under suspended loads

Safety is not just a procedure to follow: it is a commitment to yourself, your colleagues and their families.

A shared responsibility

True industrial performance protects women and men, preserves the environment and guarantees reliable operations. By making prevention everyone's business, GOC is building a safer and more resilient oil industry.
