GO’TALKGO’TALK Giving a voice to our talents

Through GO’TALK, Gabon Oil Company (GOC) shines a light on the women and men who contribute every day to its activities and ambitions.

Engineers, technicians, managers and young talents open the doors to their professions, tell the story of their careers and share the challenges that drive them.

Behind every facility, every operation and every project, there are skills, experiences and convictions. GO’TALK gives them a face and a voice, offering a fresh look at the people who move GOC forward and contribute to Gabon's energy future.
