1.What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your computer, tablet or phone when you visit it. It allows the website to recognise your browser for a limited period, for example to remember a preference or to count visits. A cookie contains no program and gives no access to the other files on your device.

The rules on this page also apply to similar techniques, such as the browser's local storage.

2.Cookies on this website

GOC has chosen to keep its website lean. It sets no advertising cookies, no social network cookies and no trackers used to build a profile of you. The typeface, the videos and the document reader are hosted on the website itself: displaying them sends no information to any outside service.

Only the following cookies may be set:

These cookies are necessary for the website to work, or exempt from consent because they are used only for anonymous audience measurement. That is why no banner asks for your consent when you arrive on the website. You nevertheless remain in control: you can refuse audience measurement at any time.

3.Audience measurement

To understand how the website is used and to improve it, GOC uses Matomo, an audience measurement tool installed on the website's server. The data stays with GOC and is not passed on to any outside service. Matomo is configured to meet the conditions that exempt it from collecting your consent:

  • IP addresses are anonymised as soon as they are collected.
  • The statistics are anonymous and used by GOC alone: they are neither combined with other data nor transferred to third parties.
  • Cookies expire after 13 months at the latest, and the data collected is kept for no more than 25 months.

You can nevertheless refuse this measurement. Your refusal is recorded in a cookie (mtm_consent_removed) so that it is respected on your next visits. If you clear your browser's cookies, you will need to make this choice again.

Accepted: your visits are counted anonymously.

4.Social networks and third-party content

The Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and X icons in the footer are simple links to GOC's pages: none of these networks sets a cookie unless you click. Once you are on their website, their own rules apply.

The website does not embed any content hosted by a third party, such as a video or an interactive map. Should this change, such content would only be loaded with your consent, and this page would be updated.

5.Browser settings

You can also set your browser to accept or refuse cookies, or to delete them. Refusing cookies does not prevent you from browsing the website. The steps depend on your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6.Questions and complaints

For any question about cookies or your personal data, see our privacy policy or write to us at: contact@gabonoil.com.

You can also refer the matter to the Commission nationale pour la protection des données à caractère personnel (CNPDCP), the Gabonese data protection authority. This page is updated whenever the cookies used by the website change.
