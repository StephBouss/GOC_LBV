1.Collection of personal data

GOC is committed to protecting your personal information. We only collect data that you provide to us voluntarily, in particular through the contact, recruitment and newsletter sign-up forms. The data collected generally includes:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address and telephone number
  • Your application documents (CV, cover letter)
  • Any information entered in the message fields

The search for the nearest Gab'Oil station, on the home page, uses your device's location only if you ask for it and allow it in your browser. This location is processed in your browser: it is never sent to GOC or stored. The “Directions in Google Maps” link only gives Google the destination station.

2.Use of data

Your information is processed for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes:

  • Responding to your enquiries and requests for information.
  • Processing and assessing your job applications.
  • Sending you GOC's newsletter once you have subscribed; every issue includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Improving the operation and usability of the website through anonymous browsing statistics (see the Cookie management page).

3.Data retention

GOC keeps your data only for as long as is strictly necessary to fulfil the intended purposes:

  • Application data is kept for a maximum of 2 years after the last contact.
  • Requests sent through the contact form are kept for a maximum of 3 years.
  • The email address used to subscribe to the newsletter is kept until you unsubscribe.

4.Data sharing

The personal data collected is reserved exclusively for internal use by GOC's authorised departments (Human Resources, Communications, IT). No data is sold, rented or transferred to third parties. The forms and the newsletter may be sent through a technical service provider, which acts solely on behalf of GOC and in accordance with its instructions.

If you show the Google Maps map on the Contact page, Google receives your IP address and applies its own rules. This map only loads with your consent, which you can withdraw on the Cookie management page.

5.Your rights

In accordance with data protection regulations, you have the right to access, rectify, update and delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at: contact@gabonoil.com.
