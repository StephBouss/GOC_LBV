Gabon Oil Participations

Strategic assets, proven expertise

Managing, safeguarding and adding value to the nation's oil interests: this is the business of GOP, a GOC subsidiary.

Our history

26 years of expertise, a new horizon

GOP grew out of Tullow Oil Gabon, formerly Africa Energy, which has been present in Gabon's oil basin for more than two decades. Now part of the national portfolio, this expertise serves GOC's industrial strategy and the interests of Gabon.

  • More than 26 years in Gabon's oil industry
  • In-depth knowledge of the assets and of their technical and economic environment
  • A command of the contracts that govern partnerships between companies
  • Specialist expertise in non-operated oil assets
Tullow Oil Gabon becomes GOP

Our business

Value beyond production

As a non-operating partner, GOP does not run operations in the fields. It monitors the assets, reviews technical programmes, assesses investments and tracks their economic performance.

01

Adding value to assets

Safeguarding and growing the value of its interests through active, rigorous portfolio management.

02

Analysing performance

Tracking production, costs, development programmes and results to assess the profitability of the assets.

03

Informing investment decisions

Assessing partner operators' projects and their risks, for economically sound decisions.

04

Defending the interests held

Ensuring agreements are applied and taking part in the partnerships' decision-making bodies.

05

Anticipating risks

Identifying the technical, economic and operational factors that affect the long-term value of the assets.
