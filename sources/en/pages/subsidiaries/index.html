Players across the value chain

In fifteen years, the Société Nationale des Hydrocarbures du Gabon (SNHG) has gradually built an integrated group covering the entire oil value chain. This growth rests on five complementary subsidiaries, which are now the main drivers of its development.

Acquired in June 2024, Assala Energy is a major strategic asset, producing around 57,000 barrels per day.
