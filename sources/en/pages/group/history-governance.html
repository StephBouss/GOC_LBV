History & governance

From regained sovereignty to operational strength

From Gabon Oil Company to SNHG: the journey of a company serving national sovereignty.

History

Key dates

  1. 2011

    Founding of the company (GOC)

    August 2011: Gabon Oil Company is created by Decree No. 1017/PR/MMPH, laying the first stone of energy sovereignty and national control of hydrocarbons in Gabon.

  2. 2012

    The first steps of a national operator

    November 2012: SNHG signs its first exploration and production sharing contract (CEPP) for the Remboué field. This founding act symbolises the State's gradual return to the heart of its own resources.

  3. 2013–2014

    Arrival on the Obangue field

    The company begins its first direct operations by taking over activities on the Obangue field. This stage demonstrates its ability to operate, learn, organise itself and pursue a lasting industrial ambition.

  4. October 2014

    First joint venture

    SNHG sets up a joint venture with Bourbon Gabon, a key step in bringing in new expertise and strengthening national operating capacity.

  5. September 2016

    Expansion on the Mboga II CEPP

    The company strengthens its activities by taking over operations of the Mboga II CEPP, confirming its standing as a major technical operator.

  6. December 2018

    Taking over the State's interests in oil titles

    SNHG enters into a strategic partnership to manage the State's 32 interests in the oil assets held by Perenco Oil & Gas Gabon (POGG). A major turning point: the State regains a consolidated view and stronger oversight of its asset portfolio.

  7. June 2019

    Ramping up

    The company acquires stakes in the Dussafu (BW Energy) and Ezanga (Maurel & Prom Gabon) CEPPs. SNHG gradually builds up its portfolio of strategic assets.

  8. 2023

    Recovery of 40% of the Atora CEPP

    A transfer agreement concluded with Perenco Oil & Gas Gabon enables the company to recover 40% of the Atora CEPP, strengthening its asset base and its presence in the upstream oil sector.

  9. March 2024

    Operator of the Dinonga and Irondou permits

    SNHG becomes operator of the Dinonga and Irondou permits, expanding its scope for action and taking a new step in consolidating local expertise.

  10. June 2024

    Historic acquisition: Assala Energy

    SNHG acquires 100% of Assala Energy from the US fund Carlyle on behalf of the Gabonese State. It is the most significant transaction in the sector's recent history: an immediate increase in operated volumes, stronger control of key infrastructure and a strategic repositioning of Gabon on the African energy stage.

  11. 2024–2025

    The era of consolidation and transformation

    Transfer of Addax Petroleum Gabon's assets (March 2024), takeover of the Société de Maintenance Pétrolière (SMP) and acquisition of the assets of Tullow Oil Gabon, which becomes Gabon Oil Participations.

  12. 2025

    Structure, governance and a renewed vision

    2025 marks a decisive phase in the structuring of SNHG: internal reorganisation and stronger human capital, modernised governance, and business synergies between SNHG, Gab'Oil, SMP and the acquired assets to build a coherent ecosystem.

  13. Heading for 2030

    A sovereign and sustainable energy future

    By 2030, SNHG aims to spearhead Gabon's energy transition and sustainable industrialisation: consolidating its oil assets, deploying low-carbon technologies, training a local technical elite and diversifying into the energies of the future.

Governance

Strong governance serving the national interest

The governance of the Société Nationale des Hydrocarbures du Gabon (SNHG) is based on the principles of transparency, accountability and operational excellence. Designed to support Gabon's energy ambitions, it ensures rigorous decision-making, in line with international standards and in the national interest.
