CSR commitments

Creating value, preserving the future

For GOC, energy performance is also measured by the value created for Gabon, the opportunities offered to its people and the footprint left on the environment.

Our approach

Corporate social responsibility at the heart of our strategy

Convinced that hydrocarbon development must bring lasting benefits to the Nation, Gabon Oil Company (GOC) does not measure its performance by production volumes alone. It also measures it by:

  • the value created for Gabon;
  • the opportunities offered to local people;
  • the footprint left on the environment.

This ambition is reflected in five complementary policies, united by the same requirement: making every oil activity a lever for responsible, inclusive and sustainable development.

Our five commitment policies

Five policies, one shared requirement

Governance, the environment, local content, human capital and community progress: each policy sets out concrete, structured and measurable commitments.

01

Corporate social responsibility

Creating lasting, shared value.

Economic performance, respect for the environment and social progress go hand in hand, so that local people benefit from industrial development.

  • Listening to stakeholders' expectations
  • Quality and safety of the products we distribute
  • Support for the health of neighbouring communities
  • Dialogue and handling of complaints
02

Ethics, compliance and transparency

Making integrity the foundation of trust.

Responsible governance, based on setting an example, risk prevention and accountability: trust is built through verifiable actions.

  • Prevention of corruption and fraud
  • Management of conflicts of interest
  • Stronger internal controls and reliable information
  • Protection of people who report misconduct in good faith
03

Environment

Reducing our footprint, preserving resources, preparing for tomorrow.

Preventing pollution and controlling the impact of our activities, to reconcile energy development with the preservation of Gabon's natural heritage.

  • Risk assessment and emergency preparedness
  • Reduction of flaring and greenhouse gas emissions
  • Responsible water and waste management
  • Biodiversity protection and site rehabilitation
04

Communities and local content

Turning national resources into opportunities for Gabonese people.

Local content drives economic sovereignty: Gabonese skills and companies take their full place in the oil value chain.

  • Employment and training of nationals
  • Local companies and suppliers
  • Transfer of technology and know-how
  • Community projects: health, education, water, electricity, infrastructure
05

Social policy and working conditions

Developing skills, protecting people, nurturing talent.

A respectful, fair and safe working environment, where every talent can develop their potential and contribute to the company's ambitions.

  • Fundamental rights, occupational health and safety
  • Equal opportunities and social dialogue
  • Continuous training and career development
  • Gabonese employment, internal mobility and quality of working life
