With Gab'Oil and Pizolub, GOC distributes petroleum products across the country and manufactures lubricants in Gabon.

Downstream

Distribution: bringing energy closer to people

Through Gab'Oil, its distribution and marketing subsidiary, GOC supplies and distributes petroleum products across the country. The network, which had 7 service stations in 2023, now has 13 and aims for 25. Every station shares the same mission: making energy accessible everywhere, including in remote areas.

  • A network of 13 active service stations
  • New stations opened and existing stations renovated
  • Improved service for customers
  • A lever for energy sovereignty and price regulation

Lubricants

Pizolub: lubricants made in Gabon

Based in Port-Gentil, Pizolub is the Group's industrial facility specialising in the manufacture of lubricants. After several years of slowdown, its production lines have been upgraded under GOC's leadership and now meet international industrial quality standards.

  • A lubricants plant in Port-Gentil
  • Production lines that meet international standards
  • Modernisation programmes to increase capacity
  • A local offering that meets national needs
