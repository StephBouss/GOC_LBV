As the State's operational instrument for managing the nation's oil and gas assets, GOC adds value to Gabon's gas resources.

Gas Department

Adding value to the Group's gas resources

Within GOC, the Gas Department designs and assesses solutions to add value to the Group's gas resources, in particular the gas associated with oil production, part of which may be flared. Market studies and techno-economic analyses inform strategic decisions.

  • Adding value to the gas associated with oil production
  • Making the reduction of gas flaring a major priority
  • Market studies and techno-economic analyses
  • A contribution to Gabon's Gas Transformation Acceleration Plan (PAT)

Value creation

Turning gas into value for the country

Rather than being flared, associated gas can become a resource that serves the economy and the population.

Generating electricity

Gas can fuel power generation (Gas-to-Power).

Supplying households

Liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) meets the everyday needs of Gabonese households.

Serving remote localities

Liquefied (LNG) and compressed natural gas (CNG) can supply localities not connected to the networks.

Distribution

Butane gas, close to households

As the national marketer, Gab'Oil helps meet the country's gas needs, a resource that Gabonese households cannot do without. Developing the gas business is one of its major priorities for 2026.

  • A three-year budget plan to acquire gas cylinders
  • An optimised supply and distribution chain
  • Teams trained in the sales techniques specific to gas
  • Fair, stable and lasting access to gas
