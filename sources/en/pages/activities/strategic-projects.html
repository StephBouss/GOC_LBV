Our projects

Eight projects, three strategic pillars

From oil fields to site infrastructure, each project serves one of the three pillars of GOC's strategy.

Pillar 1

Increasing production

Bringing existing fields, onshore and offshore, back to full value to increase national production.

01

Redevelopment of mature onshore fields

An infill drilling and workover programme to increase the reserves and production of onshore assets.

02

Development of the offshore Sardine field

Improving hydrocarbon recovery through water injection, workover operations and the drilling of new wells.

03

Restart of the offshore Konzi and Ayol fields

Rehabilitating existing infrastructure and bringing high-potential assets back into production.

Pillar 2

Renewing reserves

Confirming the oil and gas potential of new fields to prepare tomorrow's production.

04

Appraisal and development of Aloumbé gas

A project to confirm and develop the site's gas potential in support of national energy sovereignty.

05

Exploration of blocks DEF-3, DE2 and D4-5

Confirming the oil and gas potential of these permits to support the renewal of the Group's reserves.

Pillar 3

Modernising infrastructure

Securing logistics and improving working conditions for the teams at production sites.

06

Expansion of storage capacity at the Tsiengui and Obangué sites

Upgrading storage infrastructure to optimise production operations.

07

Construction of a steel bridge in Obangué

Improving access to the facilities and operational logistics.

08

Construction of a new living quarters base in Mboumba

Improving accommodation and working conditions for the on-site teams.

Horizon 2026: an ambition fulfilled

Through these strategic projects, GOC confirms its role as the driving force behind Gabon's energy sovereignty.
