Activities

GOC: building a national energy champion

Upstream oil: exploring, producing and growing national reserves

Upstream oil is the foundation of the Group's growth. In just a few years, GOC has become a major player in national production. Its own production, from its operated and non-operated assets, now stands at around 27,000 barrels per day.

Discover exploration and production

Downstream: from distribution to processing

Through Gab'Oil, GOC is carrying out an ambitious programme to develop its distribution network, which had 7 service stations in 2023 and aims for 25. With Pizolub, the Group has an industrial facility specialising in the manufacture of lubricants.

Discover distribution and processing

Oil services: SMP and national expertise

With the integration of SMP – Société de Maintenance Pétrolière, GOC strengthens its command of drilling, maintenance and technical support activities. This business reduces dependence on outside contractors and strengthens local content.

Discover oil services

Managing interests and creating value

Through Gabon Oil Participations, GOC rigorously manages its interests in several permits operated by international partners, to increase the share of oil wealth captured for the benefit of the country.

Commitments

Building a responsible energy future

At GOC, industrial performance goes hand in hand with our commitment to people and nature. Our approach is built on strategic pillars: HSEI, CSR, the environment, safety and communities.

Environment

  • Preserving Gabon's natural heritage and biodiversity.
  • Making the transition to cleaner energy and the reduction of gas flaring major priorities.
  • Deploying low-carbon technologies by 2030.
  • Rolling out environmentally responsible solutions to guarantee Gabon's energy independence.

CSR and communities

  • Contributing to the well-being of Gabonese communities and promoting sustainable development in all our areas of operation.
  • Acting as a trusted partner and a catalyst for local development.
  • Investing continuously in the training and upskilling of Gabonese managers.
  • Developing local content and training a national technical elite.
Our CSR commitments

Governance and transparency

  • Governance founded on transparency, accountability and operational excellence.
  • Transparency (EITI): good governance reports and transparency of resources.
  • An Audit & Internal Control Committee that strengthens transparency and ensures risk control and the compliance of operations.
  • A Contracts & Procurement Committee that guarantees responsible and efficient purchasing.

HSEI: health and safety

  • Guaranteeing the complete integrity of people and industrial facilities.
  • Making the health and safety of our employees and partners the fundamental pillars of our operations.
  • Applying rigorous HSEI protocols at every site to keep everyone safe.
  • A QHSEI Committee that ensures compliance with standards and the protection of people, facilities and the environment.
