Who we are

SNHG: driving energy sovereignty

The Société Nationale des Hydrocarbures du Gabon (SNHG), of which Gabon Oil Company (GOC) is an integral part as its operational arm, is the State's operational instrument for managing the nation's oil and gas assets. Founded in August 2011, it embodies Gabon's sovereign choice to take charge of its energy future.

  • Missions

    Managing the State's interests in oil activities, marketing petroleum products and operating hydrocarbon fields on behalf of the Nation.

  • Vision

    Building a strong and dynamic national hydrocarbons company that makes a lasting contribution to the economy and development of the Gabonese Republic.

  • Values

    Respect, integrity, excellence, teamwork, profitability and innovation.
